# Generic rubric (100 points)

| Criterion | Points | Evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Scientific correctness | 30 | Numerical checks, correct analytic reference |
| Reproducibility | 25 | Commands, versions, consistent outputs |
| HPC concepts | 20 | Scheduler/resources explained accurately |
| Resource stewardship | 15 | Proportional requests, acknowledges node costs |
| Communication | 10 | Clear explanation and figures/tables |

Do not award points solely for accessing a scarce or proprietary HPC system.
